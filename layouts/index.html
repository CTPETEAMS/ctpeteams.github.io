CTPETEAMS — Higher Team Management via C-domain

Our version of project management software

Not another Asana, not another Trello. A dashboard built the CTP way, for how CTP work actually happens — teams, tasks, and the energy moving through a project.

The problem it solves. One person can't spearhead every aspect of a project. Take any project: one person planning, building, designing, writing the copy, promoting it, scheduling the launch. It doesn't work. The project stalls — not because the vision is weak, but because one person can't carry it all. CTPETEAMS is how you bring the other people in.

The principle. The CTP energy and archetypes are the organizing layer, not decoration. Every person on a team holds a mission role, and that role is governed by their energy type. You don't just see tasks. You see the energy of the project — because in CTP, the energy is the work.

How It Works

The problem, and the fix

Regular project software tracks tasks and deadlines. CTPETEAMS tracks the team and the energy behind the work — because in CTP, the energy is the work.

1 · The projectEvery project gets a dashboard — the work, the people, and the energy flowing through it, all in one place.
2 · The teamEvery person holds a mission role, governed by their energy type. The energy-type architectures organize the team.
3 · The boardThe work — what's in progress, what's done, what's blocked. A blocked card is a blocked energy flow.
4 · The energyThe flow — where the project has momentum and where it's stuck. Clear the blockage, and the energy flows again.

What it is not

  • Not an Asana/Trello clone. Task tracking is the floor, not the product. The product is the team and the energy behind the work.
  • Not a static site. This portal is the clickable prototype. The working app runs real teams on real data.
